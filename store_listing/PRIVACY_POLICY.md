# Privacy Policy for Cyclus

**App:** Cyclus (Android)

## Overview
Cyclus is a cycle tracking app designed to work **fully offline** on your device. We built Cyclus so your intimate health data stays under your control.

## Data we store on your device
Depending on how you use the app, Cyclus may store locally:
- Profile name / username and local password (for unlocking the app on this device)
- Cycle dates, period logs, symptoms, mood, and notes
- Tracking mode (period, conception, or pregnancy) and pregnancy details you enter
- Theme preferences, background photo path, and notification settings
- Optional partner PIN / locally shared cycle export files you create

This information is saved using on-device storage (for example SharedPreferences and app documents). It is **not uploaded to Cyclus servers**, because Cyclus does not operate a cloud backend for your cycle data.

## Data we do not collect
Cyclus does **not**:
- Require a Google / social login for core tracking
- Sell your personal or health data
- Show third-party advertising networks inside the app
- Sync your cycle history to a Cyclus cloud account

## Permissions
Depending on your Android version and settings, Cyclus may ask for:
- **Notifications** — to send optional period / fertile / pregnancy reminders you enable
- **Exact alarms / schedule exact alarm** — so reminders can fire on time when you allow them
- **Photos / files (via system picker)** — only when you choose a background image or import/export a partner share file
- **Vibrate / wake lock / boot completed** — to support reliable local notifications

Cyclus uses these permissions only for the features above.

## Sharing
You may optionally share information yourself:
- Export/import a cycle JSON file
- Share a partner access code / PIN on the same phone or with a file you send

Anything you share outside the app (for example through another messaging app) is under your control and that other app’s policies.

## Children’s privacy
Cyclus is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the app if you are under the applicable age.

## Medical disclaimer
Cyclus provides educational tracking and estimates only. It is **not** a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Predictions can be wrong. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, contraception, fertility, or pregnancy care.

## Data retention & deletion
Because data is stored on your device, you can remove it by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling Cyclus. Uninstalling deletes local app data on that device (unless your OS backs it up separately).

## Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
