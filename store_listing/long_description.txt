Cyclus is a calm, beautiful cycle companion that stays fully offline on your phone. Track periods, plan conception, or follow pregnancy — with soft themes, personalized tips, and predictions that learn from your history.

WHY CYCLUS
• Fully offline — your cycle data stays on this device
• Three modes — Period, Conception, and Pregnancy
• Smart predictions — next period as a day or a likely window
• Flower or Card view — switch the Today hero to the look you love
• Chameleon Code — colors gently shift with your phase and time of day
• Theme Studio — presets, custom colors, photo backgrounds, text styles
• Calendar & insights — see fertile days, patterns, and confidence
• Gentle reminders — period, fertile, ovulation, and pregnancy tips (optional)
• Partner share — same-phone PIN or offline file share, no cloud account required

DESIGNED TO FEEL GOOD
Soft glass panels, blossom themes, and a flower bloom or clean card for your cycle snapshot. Upload your own photo as a background and tune how vivid it looks.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Cyclus does not require an internet account. Profiles and cycle logs are stored locally. You control what you share with a partner via PIN or a file you export yourself.
