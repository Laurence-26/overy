About Cyclus

Cyclus is a cross-platform period, conception, and pregnancy tracker built with Flutter and Firebase. A single, switchable home screen adapts to whichever phase of life you're in — logging cycles, surfacing high-chance windows when you're trying to conceive, or showing baby-kick counts and trimester milestones when you're pregnant. The same Firestore data flows through all three modes, so your history travels with you as your goals change.

Predictions adapt to both regular and irregular cycles using a rolling average over the last six cycles plus a standard-deviation check, with a confidence score shown on the home screen. Reminders arrive at 3, 2, and 1 days before each phase at a user-chosen time. Partner sharing works via a 6-digit invite code — multiple partners are supported with a top-of-screen switcher.
